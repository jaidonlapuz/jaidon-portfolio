"Health is a fundamental right, shaped largely by the environments in which people live, learn, work, and play. Effective community health education requires actively removing structural barriers, building authentic community trust, and equipping individuals with practical skills to make safe, healthy choices. I strive to advocate for health equity, empower youth, and foster safe, accessible environments."
